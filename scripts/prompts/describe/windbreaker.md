## Lists

- `materials`: at most 4 items on what the jacket is made of and how to care for it, in this order: the fiber content as the sources state it, the largest share first ("100% polyester"; a shell and a lining apart: "Shell: 100% nylon; lining: 100% polyester"), taken only from the composition rows (소재, 혼용률, 재질, 섬유의 조성, under "Specifications" or "Fiber content"), never guessed from the title; then the care when a source states it (세탁방법: "Hand wash cold", "Machine wash cold"); then the origin as its own item: "Made in Korea." when the Origin line names a country, "Imported." when the Origin line says do not state. A composition row that says only "see the detail page" (상세페이지 참조) states nothing: leave the fiber out.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying a windbreaker, the most useful first: the protection the sources state (windproof, water-repellent, UV protection, fleece-lined warmth, cooling), the closure and the hood (full zip, half-zip anorak, detachable hood or sleeves), the weight and feel (lightweight, packable, stretch), the cut as the seller words it (cropped, relaxed fit, shirred waist), pockets and vents, the season (spring and fall, summer rounds, winter), the choices the buyer makes (colors, sizes: say "Choice of colors and sizes", never the sizes themselves).

## This kind of product: women's golf windbreakers

- Say in the intro that it is a women's golf windbreaker or jacket, with the brand, the kind (windbreaker, anorak, swing jacket) and the one feature that sets it apart (windproof, detachable sleeves, fleece lining). Unisex: say it is a unisex jacket.
- Never state a size, a size range or a measurement: no Korean size (55, 66, 77, 88), no letter size (S, M, L, XL), no "one size", no length, chest, shoulder or sleeve in cm or inches, no weight. The shop shows the size chart apart. Fit words are fine ("relaxed fit", "true to size" when buyers or the seller say so).
- Never claim waterproof, windproof or UV protection that no source states; "water-repellent" is not "waterproof".
- Write "jacket", never "jumper" (in American English a jumper is a dress).
- A department store's or a branch's name ([현대백화점], [AK수원점]) is the seller's, never the product's: leave it out.
