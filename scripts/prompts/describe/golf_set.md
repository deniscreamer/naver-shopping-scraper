## Lists

- `included`: what the buyer receives. One item per group of clubs in the order of the bag (driver, fairway woods, hybrids, irons and wedges, putter), then the bag and head covers. Give a loft or a club number only when "What is sold", the Title or the Specifications state it and no other source contradicts it. Give a count of clubs only when a source states it ("7i", "Total Clubs: 11"); otherwise name the clubs without counting them ("Fairway woods: #3, #5") and keep a range as the source writes it ("#5-#9"). Never list a paid extra or a gift. When the buyer picks between configurations and the contents depend on the pick, leave this list empty and name the choice in a highlight.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying, the most useful first.

## This kind of product: golf club sets

- Say in the intro who the set is for (men's or women's, right- or left-handed) and how many clubs it has, when given.
- A buyer expects a complete set to come with a putter and a bag. When the set has none, say it plainly in the intro: "No putter is included.", "No bag is included."
- When the buyer picks the flex, the shaft material or the loft, name the choice in one highlight ("Steel (S) or graphite (R) shafts."). Do not list every option value.
- The driver is said to be 비공인, SLE 부적합 or non-conforming: write the highlight "The driver does not conform to USGA and R&A rules; for recreational play only." 고반발 alone is a "high-rebound face": say nothing about conformity.
- The country of manufacture, when the Origin line names one, is one highlight ("Made in Japan."), never also in the intro.
- 아시아스펙, 일본스펙 or 일본정품 (a version made for the Asian or Japanese market) is a fact of its own, not a statement of where the product is made: it never conflicts with the Origin. Join the two in one highlight: "Japan-market model, made in China."
- Sellers shorten: "11.5L" is an 11.5° loft with an L flex, "7i" or "7L" is 7 irons (with an L flex), "4U" is a #4 hybrid. Write them out ("Driver, 11.5°, L flex", "7 irons").
