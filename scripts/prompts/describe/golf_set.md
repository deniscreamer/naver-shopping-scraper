## Lists

- `included`: what the buyer receives. One item per group of clubs in the order of the bag (driver, fairway woods, hybrids, irons and wedges, putter), then the bag and head covers. Name the clubs of a group as the seller lists them ("Irons: #5-#9, PW, SW"). Before you answer, add the clubs up: the list must agree with the number of clubs the Title or "What is sold" gives. When it does not, or the sources list the clubs differently, leave the list empty. When the buyer picks between configurations with different clubs (a men's 13-club and a women's 12-club set), leave the list empty and give each configuration's clubs in a highlight of its own ("Men's sets: driver, 2 fairway woods, 1 hybrid, 8 irons, putter."). Give a loft only when the seller's typed text, "What is sold", the Title or the Specifications state it and nothing contradicts it. Never list a paid extra or a gift.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying, the most useful first: the choices the buyer makes, what sets these clubs apart (head design, materials, who they are built for), a limitation. Not raw measurements (club lengths, weights, grip sizes), not small accessories (a name tag, stickers, tees).

## This kind of product: golf club sets

- Say in the intro who the set is for (men's or women's; right- or left-handed only when a source says it) and how many clubs it has, when the sources agree on the number.
- A buyer expects a complete set to come with a putter and a bag. When the set has none, say it in the intro in exactly these words: "No putter is included." / "No bag is included." (a putter or a bag among the seller's gifts counts as included: then say nothing).
- When the buyer picks the flex, the shaft material or the loft, name the choice in one highlight ("Steel (S) or graphite (R) shafts."). Do not list every option value. When one choice changes another fact (the S set has steel irons and a 9.5° driver), say so in that highlight.
- A set mixed from several makers: say which maker each group of clubs is from, the putter too.
- The driver is said to be 비공인, SLE 부적합 or non-conforming, or its 반발계수 (COR) is given as more than 0.83: write the highlight "The driver does not conform to USGA and R&A rules; for recreational play only." 고반발 alone is a "high-rebound face": say nothing about conformity.
- The country of manufacture, when the Origin line names one, is one highlight ("Made in Japan."), never also in the intro.
- 아시아스펙, 일본스펙 or 일본정품 (a version made for the Asian or Japanese market) is a fact of its own, not a statement of where the product is made: it never conflicts with the Origin. Join the two in one highlight: "Japan-market model, made in China."
- Sellers shorten: "11.5L" is an 11.5° loft with an L flex, "7i" or "7L" is 7 irons (with an L flex), "4U" is a #4 hybrid, "P, A, S" are PW, AW, SW. Write them out ("Driver, 11.5°, L flex", "7 irons").
