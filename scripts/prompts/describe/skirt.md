## Lists

- `materials`: at most 4 items on what the skirt is made of and how to care for it, in this order: the fiber content as the sources state it, the largest share first ("90% polyester, 10% spandex"; a shell and the inner shorts apart: "Shell: 100% polyester; inner shorts: 92% polyester, 8% spandex"), taken only from the composition rows (소재, 혼용률, 재질, 섬유의 조성, under "Specifications" or "Fiber content"), never guessed from the title; then the care when a source states it (세탁방법: "Hand wash cold", "Machine wash cold, do not tumble dry"); then the origin as its own item: "Made in Korea." when the Origin line names a country, "Imported." when the Origin line says do not state. A composition row that says only "see the detail page" (상세페이지 참조) states nothing: leave the fiber out.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying a skirt, the most useful first: the built-in inner shorts (a skort or culotte) when the sources say 속바지 / 치마바지 / 큐롯, the cut and pleats as the seller words them (pleated, A-line, flared, wrap, tennis), the length (mini, midi, long), the waist (elastic waist, zip, button), pockets, the fabric's feel and stretch, warmth for winter (fleece-lined, padded), the season, the choices the buyer makes (colors, sizes: say "Choice of colors and sizes", never the sizes themselves).

## This kind of product: women's golf skirts and culottes

- Say in the intro that it is a women's golf skirt (a skort when it has inner shorts, a culotte skirt when the seller calls it 큐롯), with the brand, the kind (pleated, A-line, wrap, tennis, long) and the one feature that sets it apart (inner shorts, cargo pockets, fleece lining).
- Never state a size, a size range or a measurement: no Korean size (55, 66, 77), no letter size (S, M, L), no waist size in inches or cm (25, 26, 64, 67), no "one size", no length, waist or hip in cm or inches, no weight. The shop shows the size chart apart. Fit words are fine ("true to size" when buyers or the seller say so).
- Never claim warmth, windproofing, UV protection or moisture-wicking that no source states.
- Write "inner shorts" for 속바지, never "underwear" or "panties".
- Naver's "Dri-Fit" checkbox is a function, not Nike's fabric: write "quick-dry" unless the brand is Nike.
- A department store's or a branch's name ([현대백화점], [AK수원점]) is the seller's, never the product's: leave it out.
