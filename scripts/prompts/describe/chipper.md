## Lists

- `included`: what the buyer receives: the chipper in one item, with its loft and length when the sources agree ("Chipper, 37°, 35 in"), then a head cover when the seller lists it as included. When the buyer picks the loft, the length, the hand or the shaft, name the club alone ("Chipper") and the choice in a highlight. Never list a paid extra or a gift.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying, the most useful first: the choices the buyer makes (loft, length, right- or left-handed, shaft), what sets this chipper apart (double-sided for either hand, a wide or anti-dig sole, a putter-style stroke, an alignment line, who it is for, as a noun: "for beginners", "for seniors", never "golfers who want"), a limitation (non-conforming). Handedness goes in the intro, never alone as a highlight. Not raw measurements (lie angle, head weight, swing weight).

## This kind of product: chippers

- Say in the intro that it is a chipper, a single club for chip and bump-and-run shots around the green played with a putting stroke, with its loft when the sources agree. A double-sided chipper (양면, 투웨이, 양방향) is for right- and left-handed players: say so in the intro.
- A chipper-putter (겸용 퍼터, 칩퍼터) is one club: never say a putter is included.
- 비공인 means the club does not conform to the rules of golf: say it plainly as a limitation ("Non-conforming: not for competition play."); a double-sided head is non-conforming only when a source says so. 공인 / USGA 규정 means it conforms.
- The country of manufacture, when the Origin line names one, is a highlight of its own ("Made in Japan."); never join it with any other point. 일본스펙 or 아시아스펙 (a Japan- or Asia-market model) is a fact of its own: join it only with the origin ("Japan-market model, made in China.").
- Sellers write lofts as "35도" (35°) and lengths as "34인치" (34 in); "35도 / 50도" are two lofts the buyer chooses between, not one club.
