## Lists

- `included`: what the buyer receives. For a set, one item per group of clubs in the order of the bag (driver, woods, hybrids, irons and wedges, putter), then the bag and head covers, as the seller lists them. Before you answer, add the clubs up: the list must agree with the number the Title or "What is sold" gives; when it does not, leave the list empty. For a single club, one item: the club. Never list a paid extra or a gift.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a parent checks before buying, the most useful first: the age or height the club or set is made for, the choices the buyer makes (size, left- or right-handed, color), what sets it apart (light weight, materials), a limitation. Not raw measurements of each club.

## This kind of product: junior golf clubs

- Say in the intro whether it is a single club or a set, how many clubs a set has when the sources agree, and the age or height range the seller gives. Never guess an age or a height the sources do not state.
- When the buyer picks a size by age or height (S / M / L, 54 / 57 / 60), name the choice and what each size fits in one highlight, as the seller states it.
- A plastic or toy club, or one the seller calls 연습용 (for practice), is for practice: say so in the intro.
- A set that has no bag or no putter, when the sources say so: "No bag is included." / "No putter is included." Say nothing when the sources do not tell.
- The country of manufacture, when the Origin line names one, is one highlight ("Made in China.").
