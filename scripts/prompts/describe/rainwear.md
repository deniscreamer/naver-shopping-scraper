## Lists

- `materials`: at most 4 items on what the rainwear is made of and how to care for it, in this order: the fiber content as the sources state it, the largest share first ("100% polyester"; a shell and the lining apart: "Shell: 100% nylon; lining: 100% polyester mesh"), taken only from the composition rows (소재, 혼용률, 재질, 섬유의 조성, under "Specifications" or "Fiber content"), never guessed from the title; then the care when a source states it (세탁방법: "Hand wash cold", "Do not iron"); then the origin as its own item: "Made in Korea." when the Origin line names a country, "Imported." when the Origin line says do not state. A composition row that says only "see the detail page" (상세페이지 참조) states nothing: leave the fiber out.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying rainwear, the most useful first: what keeps the rain out as the seller words it (waterproof, water-repellent, seam-sealed, a membrane), breathability or vents, what comes in the box (a jacket and pants, a carry pouch), the hood, the length and cut (short, half, long, poncho, cape, skirt), packability and weight words, the choices the buyer makes (colors, sizes, styles: say "Choice of colors and sizes", never the sizes themselves).

## This kind of product: golf rainwear

- Say in the intro what it is (a rain jacket, rain coat, rain suit, poncho or rain skirt) for men, women or unisex as the sources state, with the brand and the one feature that sets it apart.
- Never state a size, a size range or a measurement: no letter size (S, M, L), no Korean size (90, 95, 100), no "one size", no length, chest or waist in cm or inches, no weight in grams. The shop shows the size chart apart. Fit words are fine ("true to size", "roomy enough over golf wear" when buyers or the seller say so).
- Never claim waterproofing, a waterproof rating (mm), breathability, windproofing, seam sealing, GORE-TEX or UV protection that no source states; "water-resistant" (생활방수) is not "waterproof".
- A rain suit is a jacket and pants sold together: say so; a listing that sells a coat or a suit as choices says both.
- A department store's or a branch's name ([현대백화점], [AK수원점]) is the seller's, never the product's: leave it out.
