## Lists

- `included`: what the buyer receives: the irons and wedges of the set in one item, as the seller lists them ("Irons: #6-#9, PW, AW, SW"), then head covers or other parts of the set when the seller lists them. Before you answer, add the clubs up: the list must agree with the number of irons the Title or "What is sold" gives; when it does not, or the buyer picks between sets of different clubs (5, 6 or 7 irons), leave the list empty and name the choice in a highlight. Never list a paid extra or a gift.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying, the most useful first: the choices the buyer makes (number of irons, shaft, flex), what sets these irons apart (head design, forged or cast, materials, who they are built for, as a noun: "for beginners", "for slower swing speeds", never "golfers who want"), a limitation. Handedness goes in the intro, never alone as a highlight; a head type alone ("Cavity back head.") is no highlight: say what it does or leave it out. Not raw measurements (club lengths, weights, lofts of each iron).

## This kind of product: women's iron sets

- Say in the intro that it is a women's iron set and how many irons it has, when the sources agree on the number.
- When the buyer picks the shaft material, the flex or the number of irons, name the choice in one highlight ("Graphite shafts, L or A flex."). Do not list every option value.
- The country of manufacture, when the Origin line names one, is a highlight of its own ("Made in Japan."); never join it with any other point. 일본스펙 or 아시아스펙 (a Japan- or Asia-market model) is a fact of its own, written without 정품 or 병행: join it only with the origin ("Japan-market model, made in China.").
- When the buyer's choice only adds a club (a #5 iron or a hybrid) to the same irons, list the irons every set has and name the added club in a highlight. Write each wedge by its name (PW, AW, GW, UW, SW) or its loft ("50° wedge") as the source gives it.
- Sellers shorten: "7i" is 7 irons, "6-SW" is #6 to SW, "P, A, S" are PW, AW, SW, "PA" is PW and AW, a bare loft after PW ("50, 56") is a wedge ("50° and 56° wedges"). Write them out.
