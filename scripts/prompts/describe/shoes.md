## Lists

- `materials`: at most 4 items on what the shoe is made of and how to care for it, in this order: the upper and the sole as the sources state them ("Upper: synthetic leather and mesh", "Outsole: rubber"), taken only from the material rows (소재, 제품의 주소재, 갑피, 밑창, under "Specifications"), never guessed from the title; then the care when a source states it; then the origin as its own item: "Made in Korea." when the Origin line names a country, "Imported." when the Origin line says do not state. A material row that says only "see the detail page" (상세페이지 참조) states nothing: leave it out.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying golf shoes, the most useful first: the BOA dial closure (how it tightens and releases, as the seller words it), spikeless grip and where the shoe can be worn, waterproofing and breathability as stated, cushioning, weight and comfort words, a wide last when the shoe is sold wide, the choices the buyer makes (colors, widths, sizes: say "Choice of colors and sizes", never the sizes themselves).

## This kind of product: BOA spikeless golf shoes

- Say in the intro what it is (spikeless golf shoes with a BOA dial) for men, women or unisex as the sources state, with the brand, the model and the one feature that sets it apart.
- Never state a size, a size range or a measurement: no Korean size (250, 265mm), no US size, no width in mm, no weight in grams. The shop shows the size chart apart. Fit words are fine ("true to size", "roomy toe box" when buyers or the seller say so; "runs small, consider a half size up" only when buyers say so).
- Never claim waterproofing, a warranty, GORE-TEX, a BOA Fit System model (L6, Li2, H3), or a spike count that no source states; "water-resistant" (생활방수) is not "waterproof".
- A shoe bag, insoles or socks given with the pair are a gift: say "comes with a shoe bag" only when the source says it comes with the pair.
- A department store's or a branch's name ([현대백화점], [국내매장판]) is the seller's, never the product's: leave it out.
