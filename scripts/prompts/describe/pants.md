## Lists

- `materials`: at most 4 items on what the pants are made of and how to care for them, in this order: the fiber content as the sources state it, the largest share first ("88% polyester, 12% spandex"; a shell and the lining apart: "Shell: 100% polyester; lining: 100% polyester fleece"), taken only from the composition rows (소재, 혼용률, 재질, 섬유의 조성, under "Specifications" or "Fiber content"), never guessed from the title; then the care when a source states it (세탁방법: "Hand wash cold", "Machine wash cold, do not tumble dry"); then the origin as its own item: "Made in Korea." when the Origin line names a country, "Imported." when the Origin line says do not state. A composition row that says only "see the detail page" (상세페이지 참조) states nothing: leave the fiber out.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying golf pants, the most useful first: the fit and leg as the seller words them (slim, straight, tapered, bootcut, wide-leg, jogger), the stretch, the waist (elastic, hidden elastic, belt loops), warmth for winter (fleece-lined, padded) or cooling for summer, pockets, the season, a hemming service when the seller offers one (기장맞춤), the choices the buyer makes (colors, sizes, lengths: say "Choice of colors and sizes", never the sizes themselves).

## This kind of product: men's and women's golf pants

- Say in the intro that they are men's, women's or unisex golf pants as the sources state, with the brand, the kind (stretch, jogger, slacks, bootcut, cargo, fleece-lined) and the one feature that sets them apart.
- Never state a size, a size range or a measurement: no waist size (28, 30, 32, 76, 82), no letter size (S, M, L), no Korean women's size (55, 66, 77), no "one size", no inseam, rise, length, waist or hip in cm or inches, no weight. The shop shows the size chart apart. Fit words are fine ("true to size" when buyers or the seller say so).
- Write "pants", never "trousers"; a short or long length option (숏 / 롱) is an inseam choice, never "shorts".
- Never claim warmth, windproofing, water repellency, UV protection or moisture-wicking that no source states.
- Naver's "Dri-Fit" checkbox is a function, not Nike's fabric: write "quick-dry" unless the brand is Nike.
- A department store's or a branch's name ([현대백화점], [AK수원점]) is the seller's, never the product's: leave it out.
