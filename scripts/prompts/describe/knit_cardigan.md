## Lists

- `materials`: at most 4 items on what the cardigan is made of and how to care for it, in this order: the fiber content as the sources state it, the largest share first ("70% acrylic, 30% wool"; a shell and a lining apart: "Shell: 100% wool; lining: 100% polyester"), taken only from the composition rows (소재, 혼용률, 재질, 섬유의 조성, under "Specifications" or "Fiber content"), never guessed from the title; then the care when a source states it (세탁방법: "Hand wash cold", "Dry clean only"); then the origin as its own item: "Made in Korea." when the Origin line names a country, "Imported." when the Origin line says do not state. A composition row that says only "see the detail page" (상세페이지 참조) states nothing: leave the fiber out.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying a cardigan, the most useful first: the closure (buttons, full zip, two-way zip), the neck and collar (V-neck, crew neck, high neck, hood, shawl collar), the knit and its feel (cable, textured, WHOLEGARMENT seamless, soft, lightweight), windproof or a hybrid body, the fit as the seller words it (relaxed fit, oversized, slim), pockets, the season (spring and autumn, winter warmth), the choices the buyer makes (colors, sizes: say "Choice of colors and sizes", never the sizes themselves).

## This kind of product: women's golf knit cardigans

- A men's knit of this category (a sweater, a half-zip, a pullover: the men's long-sleeve tops, 2026-11-22): say in the
  intro that it is a men's golf knit sweater (or half-zip), never a cardigan unless it opens down the front; the
  lists and the rules below hold for it as for a cardigan.

- Say in the intro that it is a women's knit cardigan for golf, with the brand, the closure and the one feature that sets it apart (windproof lining, cashmere blend, cable knit). Unisex: say it is a unisex cardigan.
- Never state a size, a size range or a measurement: no Korean size (55, 66, 77, 88), no letter size (S, M, L, XL), no "one size", no length, chest, shoulder or sleeve in cm or inches, no weight. The shop shows the size chart apart. Fit words are fine ("relaxed fit", "true to size" when buyers or the seller say so).
- A two-piece set or an ensemble is described as what is sold: the cardigan and the top, both named.
- A department store's or a branch's name ([현대백화점], [AK수원점]) is the seller's, never the product's: leave it out.
- Never call a cardigan a jacket or a sweater in the intro, even when the seller's title stacks the words ("니트 가디건 자켓", "스웨터 니트 가디건").
