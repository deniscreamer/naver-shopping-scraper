## Lists

- `materials`: at most 4 items on what the top is made of and how to care for it, in this order: the fiber content as the sources state it, the largest share first ("95% polyester, 5% spandex"; a body and a trim apart: "Body: 100% cotton; rib: 95% cotton, 5% spandex"), taken only from the composition rows (소재, 혼용률, 재질, 섬유의 조성, under "Specifications" or "Fiber content"), never guessed from the title; then the care when a source states it (세탁방법: "Hand wash cold", "Machine wash cold, do not tumble dry"); then the origin as its own item: "Made in Korea." when the Origin line names a country, "Imported." when the Origin line says do not state. A composition row that says only "see the detail page" (상세페이지 참조) states nothing: leave the fiber out.
- `highlights`: at most 4 points a golfer checks before buying a long-sleeve top, the most useful first: the neck and the closure (collar, mock neck, turtleneck, half-zip, crew neck), the warmth the sources state (fleece-lined, heat-retaining, windproof front), the fabric's feel and stretch (soft, stretch, moisture-wicking, ribbed), the cut as the seller words it (relaxed fit, slim fit, cropped, layered look), a base layer worn under another top, the season (spring and fall, winter rounds), the choices the buyer makes (colors, sizes: say "Choice of colors and sizes", never the sizes themselves).

## This kind of product: women's and men's long-sleeve golf tops

- Say in the intro that it is a women's or a men's long-sleeve golf top, as the product is, with the brand, the kind (long-sleeve T-shirt, collared polo, mock-neck top, half-zip pullover, sweater, sweatshirt, base layer) and the one feature that sets it apart (fleece lining, windproof front, a mock collar). Unisex: say it is a unisex top.
- Never state a size, a size range or a measurement: no Korean size (55, 66, 77, 88, 95, 100, 105), no letter size (S, M, L, XL), no "one size", no length, chest, shoulder or sleeve in cm or inches, no weight. The shop shows the size chart apart. Fit words are fine ("relaxed fit", "true to size" when buyers or the seller say so).
- Never claim warmth, windproofing, UV protection or moisture-wicking that no source states.
- A base layer (이너) is worn under another top: say so; never call it underwear.
- Naver's "Dri-Fit" checkbox is a function, not Nike's fabric: write "quick-dry" unless the brand is Nike.
- A department store's or a branch's name ([현대백화점], [AK수원점]) is the seller's, never the product's: leave it out.
